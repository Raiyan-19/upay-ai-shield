Responsible AI & Regulatory Governance Framework

Enterprise safety mandates, human-in-the-loop controls, and ethical safeguards governing the upay AI Shield platform.

Compliance Metrics JSON
1

Strict Human-in-the-Loop

The AI system serves solely as decision-support. No autonomous freezing of funds or accounts is ever permitted. Qualified human fraud analysts hold sole consequential authority.

2

Mathematical Explainability (XAI)

Every risk score is decomposed into local SHAP attributions (+Δ increasing points, -Δ mitigating points) so analysts and auditors know the exact reason behind every alert.

3

Customer Data Privacy & Tokenization

All customer records use cryptographic tokenized IDs (CUST00001 – CUST05000). No plaintext national IDs, phone numbers, or credentials enter the inference engine.

4

Anti-Bias & Geographic Inclusivity

Surveillance thresholds are rigorously audited across all 64 districts in Bangladesh and across both APP and USSD modalities to prevent disparate demographic impact.

5

Continuous Statistical Auditing

Automated drift telemetry checks feature distribution shifts across transaction batches, flagging anomalies before they can cause operational degradation.

6

Statutory Regulatory Traceability

Every case triage, note, status transition, and formal decision is timestamped and recorded in immutable audit logs, ready for BFIU statutory compliance exports.
