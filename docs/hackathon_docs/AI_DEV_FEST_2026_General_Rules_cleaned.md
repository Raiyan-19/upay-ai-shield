## PAGE 3

AI DEV FEST 2026 Official Rulebook 2 1  Registration, Identification and Kits 1.1 Participants must complete official registration using their real name and valid student or institutional identification. 1.2 Participants must carry and present their valid institutional ID card to enter the competition venue. Participants without a valid institutional ID card will not be allowed to participate. 1.3 Participants must be physically present at the event to receive their kits. No alternative collection or courier option will be available. Participants who are not present will not receive their kits. 2  Devices and Internet Access 2.1 Devices will be provided for Prompt Battle, Vibe Coding and Datathon. Among these competitions, participants may bring their own devices only for Vibe Coding. 2.2 Internet access will be available for participants using personal devices; however, network speed and stability cannot be guaranteed. Participants bringing their own devices are requested to bring a backup internet connection, such as a modem or mobile hotspot. 3  Reporting Time and Late Entry 3.1 Solo competitions (Vibe Coding, Prompt Battle and Datathon): Participants must report to their assigned venue on time. Late entry will be allowed for up to 10 minutes after the competition starts. No entry will be permitted after 10 minutes, and no additional time will be granted for late entry. 3.2 AI Hackathon: Participants may join up to 1 hour after the competition starts. However, no additional time or deadline extension will be provided for late entry.

AI DEV FEST 2026 Official Rulebook 3 4  Fair Participation and Original Work 4.1 No participant or team may receive external human assistance during an active competition. Any participant or team found receiving such assistance will be immediately disqualified, and their participation in that competition will be cancelled. 4.2 Sharing prompts, code, solutions, files, datasets, answers, credentials or contest information with another participant or team is strictly prohibited. 4.3 Account sharing, impersonation or participating on behalf of another person is strictly prohibited. 4.4 Submissions must be the participant's or team's own competition work. Copying another participant's solution or submission is strictly prohibited. 5  AI Tools and Account Use 5.1 There is no limitation on the use of AI tools. Participants may use either free or Pro (paid) versions during the competition. 5.2 Participants are responsible for access to their chosen AI tools. The organizers are not responsible for downtime, rate limits, login issues, subscription restrictions or exhausted credits. 5.3 Participants must use their own AI accounts. Sharing or using another participant's AI account is strictly prohibited. 5.4 Participants are responsible for everything they submit, including content or code produced using AI. 5.5 AI tools must not be used to obtain leaked or private contest materials, access restricted information, impersonate others or bypass contest rules. 5.6 If requested by the judges, participants must disclose the AI tools or models used and show relevant prompt or development history.

## PAGE 5

AI DEV FEST 2026 Official Rulebook 4 6  Submissions and Deadlines 6.1 Participants are responsible for checking that their final submission is complete, accessible and submitted in the required format before the deadline. 6.2 Late submissions will not be accepted unless the organizers announce an extension because of an official technical problem. 7  Conduct, Verification and Technical Issues 7.1 Participants must follow the instructions of the organizers, invigilators and judges throughout the competition. 7.2 Participants must not intentionally manipulate, exploit, jailbreak, reverse-engineer or interfere with the competition platform, evaluation system, hidden tests, system prompts or judging infrastructure. 7.3 Participants must not interfere with the contest network, submission system, devices or other participants. 7.4 Participants may be asked to show their screen, device, prompt history, code, notebook, repository or other relevant work for integrity verification. 7.5 Any technical problem must be reported to the organizers immediately during the competition. 7.6 Harassment, abusive behaviour, deliberate disruption, cheating, plagiarism or other unfair conduct may result in disqualification.

## PAGE 6

AI DEV FEST 2026 Official Rulebook 5 8  Submission and Judging 8.1 Only submissions made through the official submission method within the announced deadline will be evaluated. 8.2 The competition-specific problem statement will define the required submission format and judging criteria. 8.3 Judges may use automated evaluation, hidden tests, rubric-based evaluation, live demonstration or technical questioning, depending on the competition. 8.4 Any leaderboard or preliminary result is unofficial until the organizers complete final verification. 8.5 In the event of a tie, the competition-specific tie-breaking rule announced by the judges will apply. 9  Violations and Disqualification 9.1 A participant or team may receive a warning, score penalty, submission rejection or disqualification, depending on the seriousness of the violation. Violations include: • Cheating or unauthorized collaboration. • Plagiarism or copying another participant’s work. • Account sharing or impersonation. • Using prohibited resources or assistance. • Falsifying submission or verification evidence. • Interfering with contest systems or other participants. • Refusing to follow reasonable contest-integrity instructions. • Serious misconduct or rule violations.

AI DEV FEST 2026 Official Rulebook 6 10  Clarifications, Technical Problems & Complaints 10.1 Questions about a problem or rule must be sent through the official clarification channel or to the designated contest official. 10.2 If a clarification affects all participants, the organizers will communicate it to everyone concerned. 10.3 Technical issues must be reported immediately. Participants should not wait until the contest is over to report a problem that occurred during the contest. 10.4 Any complaint about a rule violation or contest process must be submitted through the official channel with relevant evidence. 10.5 Judges’ decisions on technical evaluation and final results are final after the official review process. 11  Final Conditions 11.1 Participants must follow this rulebook together with the official instructions for their specific competition. 11.2 If a competition-specific instruction differs from a general rule, the officially announced competition-specific instruction will apply. 11.3 The organizers may issue necessary clarifications during the event to maintain fairness and smooth operation. 11.4 Participation in AI DEV FEST 2026 means that the participant accepts the applicable competition rules and official instructions.
